# Tahsin privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Tahsin checks your Quran recitation entirely on your phone. This policy explains
what that means for your data.

## What the app uses

- **Microphone.** While a recitation session is running, the app listens to
  your recitation and turns it into text with a speech model that runs on your
  device. The audio is never sent anywhere.
- **Recordings (optional).** If "Keep recordings" is on, each session is saved
  as an audio file in the app's private storage so you can play it back. You
  can turn this off and delete recordings in Settings. Uninstalling the app
  deletes them.
- **Your progress.** Session history, mistakes, streaks, bookmarks and settings
  are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- Recitation checking works with no internet connection.

## Internet access

The app only goes online if you choose to download an extra speech model in
Settings. That download comes from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which, like
any website, receives your IP address to serve the file. Nothing about you or
your recitations is sent.

## Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, your session history and settings
may be included in your Google account backup, under Google's own policy.
Recordings and speech models are never backed up.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
